Kanjivaram silk

Six weaves, six places

Each one behaves differently on the body. If you are not sure what suits you, send us a message and we will talk it through.

Kanjivaram

Heavy mulberry silk from Tamil Nadu, with the border woven separately and joined by hand. Holds a pleat for years.

Banarasi

Varanasi silk with dense zari work. The katan ones are light enough for a long evening.

Jamdani

Fine Bengal cotton where the motif is added thread by thread at the loom. Takes weeks for one piece.

Chanderi

Silk-cotton from Madhya Pradesh. Sheer, glassy, and the easiest of the lot to wear in summer.

Tussar

Wild silk with a grainy texture and a warm natural gold. Good for block prints and hand painting.

Handloom cotton

Everyday sarees in khadi and mul. Soften with every wash and cost the least to keep.

How an order works

No cart, no checkout. You tell us what you want, we send real photos and a video of the drape, and only then do you pay.

Tell us the occasion

Message us on WhatsApp with the event, your budget and a colour you like. Nothing more is needed to start.

See it on video

We film the actual piece in daylight so you know the true colour, and send the weaver's details with it.

Pay and it ships

UPI or bank transfer. Dispatch the same day, insured, with a seven-day return if the saree is unworn.
